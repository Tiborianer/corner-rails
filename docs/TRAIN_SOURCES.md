This is a visual review candidate, not yet a verified production roster replacement. In particular, it is not a Siemens Desiro HC. The eventual roster mapping and final class designations for the intermediate/control cars remain approval decisions.

## Approved Blender production asset: Taurus 1116 + Nightjet new generation

The supplied Taurus corner, side and front-side references were rechecked for N2's geometry-only red/silver side sweep and cab-end colour blocks; no photographed pixels are shipped.

The formation does not mix the new-generation control car with old Nightjet stock. ## Private Blender review candidate: DB ICE 3 Class 403 redesign

The user supplied five ICE 3 appearance references on **2026-08-22**. They are recorded by filename in the manifest and are neither copied nor embedded. The I3 model uses original geometry and colour blocking for the long rounded Class 403 nose, panoramic black cab mask, dipping red nose stripe, smoked window band, underbody fairings and distributed roof equipment.

DB's official [ICE 3 vehicle page](https://www.deutschebahn.com/de/ICE-3-7033052) records the 200 m BR403/406 platform, 16 powered axles, 330 km/h maximum and the redesign from Bordbistro to full Bordrestaurant. The official [BR403 emergency sheet](https://www.deutschebahn.com/resource/blob/12723788/819f9bc39b4e262b31c18cfd132b4654/DB-403_04_2023-data.pdf) supplies the exact eight-car technical order: powered 403.0 end car, 403.1 transformer car, powered 403.2 converter car, 403.3 middle car, 403.8 middle/service car, powered 403.7 converter car, 403.6 transformer car and powered 403.5 end car. DB's [restaurant redesign material](https://www.deutschebahn.com/de/presse/suche_Medienpakete/5-Schoener-speisen--6854504) records its 20 seats.

The candidate uses the widely documented 25.835 m end-car and 24.775 m middle-car dimensions for a modeled 200.32 m formation. Car 403.3 has its own dining-window, blank galley-panel and roof-HVAC rhythm; cars 403.1 and 403.6 have separate raised/folded pantograph arrangements. Fine geometry is slightly exaggerated for the locked isometric camera, while wheel treads remain at standard gauge, wheel bottoms remain at Z=0 and the raised collector reaches 5.5 m. The existing production ICE 3 stays on its legacy asset until the user explicitly approves I3.

### I3 V2 approved cab and full-formation review

The V2 rebuild starts over from a measured 403.0 end car instead of shrinking a generic coach section into a nose. The Blender master records normalized guides against the documented 25.835 m end-car length and 2.95 m width, with separate longitudinal stations for the floor, beltline, shoulder and roof crown. Checkpoint 7 uses a long low multi-ring nose ending in a smaller lowered rounded tip, one continuous blue-grey windscreen raycast directly onto the shell below its matte aerodynamic surround, an angular swept four-pane side cab band, and paired compact front-centre lamp/grille panels. The windscreen layers and wipers follow the cab's local surface normals at millimetre-scale offsets rather than floating on a separate plane. The body retains the lower smoked passenger-glass band and tall single-leaf pressure-tight passenger door; its red stripe stays low, narrows toward the tip and wraps across the front instead of curling upward.

After the user approved checkpoint 7, the approved cab was preserved unchanged and reused at both ends of the complete eight-car formation. The remaining vehicles follow the official Class 403 role order: transformer cars 403.1/403.6, converter cars 403.2/403.7, middle/Bordrestaurant car 403.3 and middle/service car 403.8. The 403.3 has a distinct dining/galley window rhythm and the redesigned 20-seat restaurant; the transformer cars use separate raised and folded pantograph arrangements. The supplied files remain filename-only research records. The GLBs embed one original procedural 2048×512 atlas for stripe edges and fine vent/hatch detail; they contain no operator logo, copied photograph or externally loaded image. The private `?trainLab=ice3-br403-v2` route now exposes the full formation. V1 and the production legacy ICE 3 remain unchanged until the user separately approves the complete V2.

### I3 C two-car body-continuity checkpoint

The follow-up `ice3-br403-v2-continuity` candidate isolates the 403.0 end car and adjacent 403.1 transformer car. Both are generated from one measured 2.95 m Class 403 cross-section and one vehicle datum. Both vehicles use the same black glazing-band height and depth, inset smoked panes, tall pressure-tight doors, pearl-white body material and 0.18 m red stripe on the slightly lower 1.84 m datum. The nose sweep begins from that same shared stripe datum. The supplied `ice_3_front_sideview.jpg.avif` was rechecked for the section between the first passenger door and the windscreen: I3 C now uses one dark curved backing strip whose five closely spaced panes begin on the passenger-window datum and taper gradually into the windscreen. The checkpoint uses the same official DB Class 403 dimensions and roles cited above. Its manifest records the supplied filenames only; no supplied pixels, protected logo or external texture are shipped. The previous eight-car V2 and production asset remain protected baselines.

### I3 U complete unified formation

After approval of I3 C, the `ice3-br403-v2-unified` asset extends the same measured cross-section, 2.48 m glazing-band centre, 1.84 m stripe centre and shared materials through all eight official Class 403 roles. The 403.2/403.7 converter cars receive their own window and traction-equipment rhythm; 403.8 carries the service-area treatment; 403.1/403.6 retain raised and folded transformer-car pantographs; and 403.3 keeps distinct dining and galley sides plus its kitchen roof equipment. The two end cars reuse the approved cab and side-ribbon construction at opposite ends. Following user approval, this exact formation was promoted to normal gameplay as the production Tier 4 ICE 3 while the earlier models remain available as rollback/review baselines.

The front lenses use an original emissive material with strength preserved through `KHR_materials_emissive_strength`. One scene-owned combined spot light is attached to the moving leading cab in the React Three Fiber review laboratory, so the two physical lamps produce one authentic overlapping pool on track and ground rather than three staged patches. The laboratory now uses the same ACES tone mapping as production, a soft uniform warm platform fill for the train, and four deliberately weak ground pools beneath the visible fixture stands. This prevents the thin door panels from flashing more brightly than the carriage shells as they pass each lamp. This follows the established ownership boundary: Blender supplies geometry and emissive lens materials, while React Three Fiber supplies mobile-budgeted environment lighting. I3 U remains a private approval candidate; no production train definition or gameplay behavior changes in this pass.

The original procedural assets intentionally omit protected logos, but each GLB now includes the entire representative formation and encodes the train-family cues visible in the primary records:

- **Tier 1:** the RS1 is a single long chamfered railcar with Stadler’s characteristic trapezoidal side-window lattice, red door and compact roof equipment; Class 642 uses a separate two-ended articulated Desiro body with a rounded wraparound black cab, red shell, silver window ribbon and articulation bellows; LINT 41 uses a longer two-ended body with a distinctly flatter cab, broad windshield, two door groups and larger roof HVAC equipment.
- **Tier 2:** Desiro HC mixes high-capacity end cars with double-deck centre cars; metronom uses a blue/yellow electric locomotive, double-deck trailers, and control coach; Talent 2 uses its bulbous dark “hamster cheek” nose.
- **Tier 3:** FlixTrain is a green electric locomotive with refurbished single-deck trailers; IC1 uses a locomotive-led single-deck formation; IC2 uses a modern locomotive with double-deck trailers and a control end.
- **Tier 4:** ICE 2 has the older wedge power head and paired windscreen; ICE 3 has a smooth rounded continuous mask; ICE 4 has a longer angular nose, squarer glass, black side mask, and red belt.
- **Tier 5:** Railjet uses the approved Blender classic and new-generation formations. Nightjet uses the approved eight-vehicle Taurus 1116 + Viaggio Next Level N2 formation with weighted push-pull orientation.
